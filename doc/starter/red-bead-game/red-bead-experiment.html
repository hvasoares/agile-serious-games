What it proves

The bin is the system, and a worker has no influence over the red beads — so the spread is common-cause variation, centred near 10 (20% of 50). Ranking the pullers is ranking noise.

Green group changes the system: the count truly falls and breaks below the limit — real, detectable improvement. Sorting lowers the number at rising cost while the bin stays 20% red. Theater — slogans, bonuses, quotas, tampering — moves nothing, and tampering makes it worse.

To cut red beads you change the system, and only management can do that. That is the entire lesson.

W. Edwards Deming used this demonstration to teach that most variation belongs to the system, not the people in it. Theoretical 3σ limits assume a binomial process; with a 50-hole paddle at 20% red that's centre 10.0, UCL 18.5, LCL 1.5. Limits and centre shift when you change the paddle or supplier.
